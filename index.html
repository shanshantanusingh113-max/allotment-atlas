The problem

Every pre-buy is a bet on perishable stock

Travel distributors like TBO can lock in hotel rooms and flight seats at contract rates months ahead. Get it right and you own the inventory everyone wants. Get it wrong and you pay for empty rooms.

Buy too little7%

of demand in the history hit "sold out". Those agencies were ready to pay and booked elsewhere.

Buy too much₹4,500

lost on every unsold Goa room-night over New Year. Rooms cannot be sold after the night has passed.

Buy at the wrong time+36%

supplier rate in the final week compared with buying 60 days out. Buy early and you are guessing.

How it works

Five steps from search logs to a signed buy list

Each step runs in the Python engine and again in your browser, and both give the same answer to the rupee.

1Forecast

Weekly P10, P50 and P90 demand for every destination, learned from searches, bookings, sell-outs and a festival calendar.

8.5% error on unseen weeks
2Size

The newsvendor rule turns each forecast and the block's price, cost and recovery into the best quantity.

22 candidate blocks sized
3Time

A buy-day curve trades rising supplier rates against forecast uncertainty that shrinks as the date nears.

14 contracts to sign this week
4Choose

An exact optimiser picks the mix and sizes that earn the most for the risk you accept, under the budget and a per-destination cap.

+₹39.6 L vs a simple rule
5Stress-test

Ten thousand correlated seasons show the bad-season outcome, the worst 5%, and the chance of a loss.

bad season ₹1.95 Cr
