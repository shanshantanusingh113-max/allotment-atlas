05 · The core idea

Every pre-buy is a newsvendor bet

A hard allotment is perishable stock. Buy too few rooms and you sell out and lose margin; buy too many and unsold nights become a loss. The optimal quantity comes from the critical ratio, which trades the profit on a sale against the loss on an unsold unit. Try it.

critical ratio = (p − c) / (p − s)
Q* = μ + σ · Φ⁻¹(critical ratio)

Demand forecast for the block: μ = 120 rooms, σ = 35. In the real system μ and σ come from the forecast engine, per hotel and stay date.

–sell-out chance
How to say itIf a sale earns a lot and an unsold room costs little, buy more than average demand. If an unsold room hurts, buy less. The slider shows exactly how much.

07 · System architecture

Four layers, one decision

Data flows down from raw signals to a budget-constrained buy list. Each layer can be demoed on its own, so the team can build in parallel.

Layer A · Data

Synthetic data generator

Two years of searches, bookings, cancellations and sell-outs for 10 destinations, with seasonality and festival shocks baked in.

python · numpy · faker

External signals

Festival and holiday calendar, school breaks, long weekends, FX rate, visa rule changes, fuel index.

holidays lib · CSV feeds

Supplier rate cards

Contract price by lead time, hard vs soft allotment terms, release days.

DuckDB tables
Layer B · Intelligence

Demand forecaster

Pickup model on booking curves plus a gradient-boosted model with calendar and search features. Outputs P10/P50/P90 per destination and stay week.

LightGBM quantile · Prophet

Look-to-book signal

Search volume that does not convert is unmet demand. A rising search-to-book gap flags inventory to target.

pandas

Price and cancellation model

Price elasticity per segment and cancellation probability by lead time and refund policy.

logistic regression
Layer C · Decision

Opportunity scorer

Expected margin, sell-through probability and risk for each candidate block.

newsvendor per block

Portfolio optimizer

Picks blocks and quantities to maximize expected profit minus a risk penalty, within the budget and per-destination caps.

OR-Tools CP-SAT · PuLP

Risk simulator

10,000 Monte Carlo demand draws give the profit distribution, P10 downside and CVaR.

numpy vectorized
Layer D · Experience

Command dashboard

Demand heatmap, budget slider, Sankey of spend, efficient frontier, buy-timing curve.

React · Recharts · Nivo · deck.gl

Explain-my-pick

An LLM turns optimizer output into plain sentences a buyer can act on. It explains; it never decides.

LLM over JSON output

API

Endpoints for forecast, optimize and simulate so the engine plugs into TBO tools.

11 · Data plan

No dataset is given. Build one judges trust.

The synthetic generator is the foundation. Make its assumptions explicit and plant patterns the models must find, then show that they found them.

Validation you will show

  • Walk-forward backtest: train to month 18, forecast months 19–24
  • Forecast error (MAPE, pinball loss) vs a naive last-year baseline
  • Backtested profit: optimizer picks vs "buy last year's volume"

Research grounding

  • Pickup models on clustered booking curves beat classic pickup in published hotel studies
  • Report-date × stay-date "demand surface" framing unifies pace and pickup
  • Constrained newsvendor is the standard model for budget-limited perishable buys

13 · Judging

The four-minute demo

Every beat maps to one of the brief's five objectives.

0:00
Predict demand. Open the heatmap. Tap Goa in December and show the forecast band and the festival driver behind it.
0:50
Identify opportunities. Show the look-to-book leak: searches ending on sold-out are demand we could have served.
1:40
Optimize investments. Enter ₹5 Cr. Drag to ₹3 Cr, then ₹8 Cr, and watch the buy list and frontier re-plan live.
2:40
Maximize profit. Open one pick: recommended quantity, buy day from the timing curve, and selling price, with the LLM explanation.
3:20
Manage risk. Fire the Dubai shock. The plan shifts budget away and the P10 downside stays positive. Close on backtested profit vs baseline.

Tech stack

  • Python, pandas, DuckDB
  • LightGBM (quantile), Prophet for holiday effects
  • OR-Tools CP-SAT or PuLP for the solver
  • FastAPI backend
  • React, Vite, Recharts, Nivo Sankey, deck.gl map
  • Any capable LLM for the explainer

Risks and fixes

  • Synthetic data looks fake. Document every assumption and show the planted patterns being recovered.
  • Solver too slow live. Precompute a budget grid; use greedy as warm start.
  • Judges lost in the math. Lead with the visuals; keep formulas in an expandable panel.
  • Demo crash. Keep a recorded backup and a static JSON fallback.
